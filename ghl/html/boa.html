1 · The headline read

BOAs are your most engaged audience and your most reply-driven one.

✓ What's working

  • Engagement is excellent. Contract Sent's first email: 26 delivered, 22 opened, 7 replies. Across the flow, 12 replies in 30 days. Advisors answer when you write to them.
  • The exit logic is the best in any funnel. Every Contract Sent touch is individually gated on the boa-contract-signed tag, an entry guard skips deals already in "Signed," and a "lost" deal is removed from the workflow outright. Signers don't get chased.
  • Stop-on-reply is ON in all three workflows, and the no-show triggers cover three event tags (boa-demo-no-show, boa-intro-no-show, boa-intro-canceled).
  • SMS delivers: 64 sent, 62 delivered across the funnel in 30 days.

✗ Where it leaks

  • Zero link clicks in the contract flow — because there's nothing to click. None of the four Contract Sent emails or the SMS contains the agreement link. 68 emails delivered, 0 clicks.
  • The nurture dies at Day 12. Signed advisors — the channel the update email itself calls "one of our strongest" — get a welcome, one update, one text, and then silence forever. No recurring loop.
  • A 40-advisor no-show cohort hit Missed Meeting and the Day-0 email has no calendar link (only the follow-ups do). 1 click and 4 replies out of ~59 emails; 10 advisors are parked mid-flow.
  • Most Contract Sent emails aren't personalized: no {{contact.first_name}}, openers like "Hey, Adam from Tile Healthcare here," subjects like "Contract - Tile Healthcare," and every sign-off is "Tile Healthcare" instead of a person. No from-name/from-email anywhere.
  • Everything hangs on manual tags. The BOA pipeline's stages trigger nothing — enrollment depends on someone applying boa-contract-sent / boa-contract-signed by hand. Forget the signed tag and the guards never trip and the nurture never starts.
  • Volume-tied compensation language: "you get compensated every time one signs on" and "equity stake plus commission on anything that closes" — the same class of language the partner compliance cleanup purged. The BOA agreement may frame this differently; the copy should match whatever counsel approved, not improvise.

3 · Pipeline coverage — where automation listens

The BOA pipeline has 6 stages. None of them trigger anything — every workflow runs on manually applied tags. Highlighted rows are stages where an advisor sits and nothing happens.

01Demo Booked
No confirmation or reminder — and this funnel just produced a 40-advisor no-show cohort. Same gap as clinics and partners, at higher volume.
02Ready to Sign
No automation between verbal yes and contract out.
03Contract Sent
The stage itself is silent; the flow fires on the manually applied boa-contract-sent tag. Works — when someone remembers.
04Signed
Silent as a stage; the boa-contract-signed tag both exits the contract chase and starts the nurture. Forget the tag and both fail quietly.
05No Show
Silent as a stage; three boa-* no-show/cancel tags cover the events.
06Lost
Contract Sent checks this stage and removes the contact from the workflow — the one place a stage is actually consulted.

6 · Recommendations

In order. BOAs reply — the fixes here are about giving those replies fewer chances to be necessary and giving everyone else something to click.

1

Put the agreement link in all five Contract Sent touches.

20 min

Use {{document.url}} (as the partner flow does) in all four emails and the SMS, and turn on click tracking. This is the highest-engagement flow in the org sending reminders with nothing to click.

2

Make the nurture recurring.

~30 min

Add a 30-day wait + go-to loop after the Day-12 SMS (the Monthly Check-in pattern): one short monthly email alternating a traction update with a direct intro ask, each ending with the same one-line ask. Advisors signed up to help; let the system keep asking.

3

Move the cal.com link to Day 0 in Missed Meeting.

10 min

Email 1 and the SMS carry 70% of the volume and have no link. Add the board-of-advisor cal.com link to both; keep the reply path as the alternative.

4

Align compensation language with the BOA agreement.

15 min + counsel check

Rewrite the two flagged sentences to whatever framing the agreement actually uses, and keep per-deal payment language out of automated copy — same discipline as the partner cleanup.

5

Personalize Contract Sent and set senders everywhere.

30 min

First-name greetings, human sign-off (Adam), real subjects, from-name/from-email on all 9 emails. The rewritten copy is in boa_drip_optimized.md, ready to paste.

6

Wire the stages, keep the tags as backup.

~30 min

Trigger Contract Sent from the "Contract Sent" stage and the signed tag from the "Signed" stage (or the document-signed event), so one forgotten manual tag can't break both the chase exit and the nurture. Then a demo confirmation/reminder mini-flow — 40 no-shows a month says it pays for itself immediately.
