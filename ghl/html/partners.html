1 · What changed since the Jul 16 captures

All four workflows were renamed and re-triggered between Jul 17 and Jul 21. The wiring is dramatically better; the copy upgrade shipped for the front half of one workflow and stopped there.

✓ Fixed in the rebuild

  • Every trigger was rebuilt. The broken Contact Tag trigger (2,650 attempts, 0 matches) and the one-off webhook are gone. Missed Meeting now fires on five event tags (intro/demo/contract no-shows and cancellations), Contract Sent on the RCM pipeline's "Contract Sent" stage plus a partnership-contract-sent tag, Partner Referral on "Waiting for Referrals" plus a waiting referral tag.
  • SMS deliverability is fixed. Jul 16: 0% of SMS delivered. Last 30 days: 21 of 21 delivered. Whatever was wrong with A2P/number setup got resolved.
  • Contract Sent actually fires now: 12 emails delivered in 30 days (vs ~3 contacts ever, before), 2 signing-link clicks, 4 partners in flight right now.
  • Stop-on-reply turned ON for Missed Meeting, Contract Sent, and Partner Referral — a partner who replies no longer keeps getting nagged.
  • The optimized referral copy shipped — for Emails 1 and 2. "How referrals work (30 seconds, one email)" and the 30-second clinic filter (with the 50+ Medicare threshold filled in) are live, word for word from the optimization doc.

✗ Still open

  • Contract Sent still runs the old corporate copy end-to-end — including the "Revenue Share Terms" FAQ email the compliance cleanup said to purge (the model is a flat monthly fee), with answers that answer nothing.
  • An email with subject "test" is live at the end of Booking Rejected — and its body promises "you get a referral fee," volume-tied payment language the compliance notes ruled out.
  • One tag starts two competing sequences: partnership-intro-canceled triggers both Missed Meeting and Booking Rejected. A canceled intro currently gets chased by two automations at once.
  • The signing link exists only in Email 1. Every reminder after it (2 emails, 2 SMS) still asks the partner to go find the first email.
  • The "exit if signed" check is decorative. It's a dead-end branch at the bottom of Contract Sent, not a workflow Goal — signers still receive every reminder along the way.
  • Partner Referral's back half is untouched: the Day-7 "please submit referrals" SMS with no way to submit, the empty Day-14 "Partner Program Update," and a Day-21 branch that does nothing on either path.
  • No from-name / from-email on any of the 14 emails, and {{contact.name}} / {{contact.first_name}} are still mixed.
  • The EHR/consultants pipeline is entirely unwired — all stage triggers point at the RCM pipeline only, so EHR and consultant deals never enter these flows by stage.
